JING-ZHANG CITY COMPLETENESS

Complete the city before adding AI to everyday life.
Boundary statement: this is a conceptual urban-design submission for the open call. SITE_BOUNDARY and the three KEY_AREA features use repository-maintained provisional rough geometry only for generation, topology checks, relative spatial relationships, visualisation and package-level recalculation. They are not statutory redlines, parcels, ownership boundaries, road redlines, regulatory plans or engineering conclusions.

Design Basis and Source List

The proposal is based on the official prequalification announcement, brief/site-package, the agent taskbook, the repository source registry and the processed fact pack. The brief gives an approximately 43.6 km² coordinated research area, an approximately 11.4 km² overall design area and three key areas totalling approximately 368.4 ha. Approved FAR, building height, density, statutory green-space controls, setbacks, ownership, utilities, fire-safety, heritage controls and road redlines remain incomplete, so conceptual buildings and areas are not presented as approved planning controls.

Three-Level Scope Framework

Coordinated research area

Study the relationship between industry, talent, innovation and long-term urban life.

Overall design area

Form continuous land-use, walking, blue-green and public-space networks.

Key areas

Close specific capability gaps in Zhongzhiyuan, AI Origin and Dazhongsi.

Coordinated Research Area: Industry and Future City Research

A world-class AI ecosystem is organised as a chain of urban capabilities: research - translation - testing - adoption - long-term living. Mechanism cases include Vector Institute, Mila, the Alan Turing Institute, AI Singapore 100 Experiments, Seoul AI Hub and Punggol Digital District. The proposal borrows organisational mechanisms, not their land area, building form or investment scale.

Overall Design Area: Urban Renewal and Regulatory-Plan-Level Urban Design

The spatial structure is one spine, six segments, six stitches and three cores. The Jing-Zhang public green spine provides north-south everyday continuity. Six completeness segments address different daily capability gaps. Six east-west stitching links express walking and transfer intent between communities, the park, campuses, innovation areas and transit/city districts. Three cores are a complete innovation campus at Zhongzhiyuan, a complete long-term community at AI Origin and a complete station-city district at Dazhongsi.

Detailed Design of Key Areas

Zhongzhiyuan

R&D, pilot testing, incubation, enterprise services, public exchange and controlled test space form a complete innovation campus.

AI Origin Community

Long-term housing, childcare/education, care, shared work, ordinary retail, green space and civic commons form a short-distance daily chain. Shuangfen Fortress is only a naming easter egg.

Dazhongsi

Station-city life, ordinary retail, culture and urban adoption coexist. Because the provisional polygon has a known location risk, it is not used to infer real engineering locations.

AI Innovation Ecosystem, Personas, and AI+ Scenarios

Five design personas: long-term residents and families; students and researchers; founders and company staff; international developers and visiting scholars; and service workers, commuters and visitors. Land Use, Building Scale, and Retain-Renovate-Demolish Strategy

Thirteen conceptual land-use zones cover residential, community service, research, culture, education, commercial service and park/green-space categories. Building prototypes test adjacency among home, learning, care, work and common life. conceptual_floors are capacity tests, not approved height, FAR or development quantities. With parcel-level existing-building, ownership, structural, fire-safety and heritage data missing, the proposal makes no final retain-renovate-demolish decisions.

Transport, Rail, Municipal Infrastructure, and Public Services

The roads layer expresses walking, cycling, greenway and rail-transfer connections only. It does not modify expressways or primary roads and does not call conceptual centre lines road redlines. Public-service priorities are childcare/education, care, staffed service, civic halls and ordinary retail. Edge compute, charging, robot maintenance and sensing infrastructure must remain subordinate to formal fire, energy, network and municipal requirements.

Blue-Green Network, Public Space, and Urban Character

The public green spine first provides walking, cycling, accessibility, cultural memory, shade, stormwater performance and free places to stay, then adds optional digital interpretation and sensing. Six civic commons follow a public-first, non-app-entry rule. Urban character does not pursue an “AI architecture look”; technological quality comes from service, maintainability and spatial organisation rather than chip motifs or screen façades.

Renewal Projects, Implementation Policy, and Phasing

Near term: C7 baseline audit, walking review, community-service gap mapping and low-impact shared-space pilots. Mid term: deepen R&D-public-service-controlled-test interfaces at Zhongzhiyuan and close daily-service and east-west connectivity gaps. Long term: deepen southern station-city, cultural and market coordination only after reliable station, ownership and transport data are available. Phasing is a research sequence, not a construction commitment.

Metrics, Area Recalculation, and Compliance Matrix

The package contains nine GeoJSON layers. Provisional boundaries, conceptual land use, buildings, roads, green space, public space and phasing are reproducible. The provisional overall polygon recalculates to roughly 11,412,825 m² for package consistency only and is not promoted to an official area. constraints.geojson intentionally remains an empty FeatureCollection with metadata naming missing official constraints and replacement actions.

Risk, Copyright, and Compliance

Main risks are the absence of official precise boundaries, known location risk in the provisional Dazhongsi key-area polygon, and missing existing-building, ownership, regulatory, municipal, fire-safety, heritage and transport surveys. Figures use locally generated graphics and public/cleared data, without commercial map tiles, remote fonts, unlicensed imagery or third-party trademarks. AI assisted research, writing, data organisation, figure creation and validation; final responsibility remains with the submitter.
